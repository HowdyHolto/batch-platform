### 2024 Burning Questions
- **Date:** January 5, 2025 | **Author:** Dom Bacani
- **Tags:** 3D Printing, Laser Engrave, Tools | **Category:** Resources
- **Q1:** How does laser engraving change color on 3D prints? IR lasers cause "foaming" (lighter). Blue diode lasers etch surface. Material + color are decisive. Always test first.
- **Material findings:** PLA (IR → foaming on most, no effect on light orange/red). PETG (more responsive than PLA). ABS (light → dark, dark → light under IR). Blue diode → melts/evaporates on most.
- **Q2:** Tool recommendations: Heat set insert press, Trapezoidal ruler, Non-magnetic tweezers (titanium + reverse-action).

### Can You Laser Engrave 3D Prints?
- **Date:** February 28, 2024 | **Author:** Dom Bacani
- **Tags:** Laser Engrave, 3D Printing | **Category:** Resources
- **Equipment:** xTool F1 (2W IR + 10W blue diode, 115mm working area)
- **Settings (IR 12pt text):** Power 90, 60mm/s, 4 passes. Engraving: Power 100, 200mm/s, 240 LPI, 4 passes.
- **PLA results:** White/red → no effect (IR). Black → sharp, light brown relief. Grey → uneven scarring.
- **ABS results:** White → strong dark marks (IR). Black → sharpest results both lasers. Yellow → reasonable both.
- **Key rule:** Material color matching laser wavelength = reduced/no effect.

## Resource / Build Pages (standalone, not journal)

### /embed-magnets — Full 8-Step Magnet Guide
1. Measure magnets (actual ≠ advertised)
2. Stack + mark polarity with X
3. Design cavities (add 0.2–0.5mm clearance, boolean subtract)
4. Position cavities in corresponding pairs on opposite halves
5. Set inset distance: 0.5mm from contact surface
6. Orient for vertical drop, note layer positions in slicer
7. Pause before sealing layer, insert with correct polarity
8. Resume + complete print

### /laser-engrave-3d-prints — Full Laser Guide
- Equipment: xTool F1 (2W IR + 10W blue diode)
- Print face-down on textured plate, min 6 solid layers on engraving side
- Software: xTool Creative Space (Lightburn compatible)
- IR settings: Power 90, 60mm/s, 4 passes (text); Power 100, 200mm/s, 240 LPI, 4 passes (engrave)
- Color/material matrix (see journal article above)

### /t7hdcasebuild — Full T7 Case Build Guide
- **Improvements over v1:** Dust sealing (rubber cord compression), two-part hinge, magnetic latch, removable TPU sleeves
- **Printing:** Main shell vertical ~105g. Lid inverted. Sleeves in TPU 95A.
- **Magnets:** 1× 60×10×5mm rect + 2× 10×5mm disc (two-stage insert process)
- **Gaskets:** Upper: 1mm solid rubber cord. Lower: 2mm hollow + 1mm solid connector.
- **Laser:** xTool F1, export 1:1 PDF from CAD, SVG in Illustrator, score outlines for crisp edges. Cut foam inserts.
- **Full hardware BOM:** M3 (22mm, 6mm screws + inserts + nylon washers), M4 (8/6/10mm screws + 7× inserts + nylon washers), aluminum spacers 4.1mm ID × 7mm OD × 4mm L, EVA foam (6mm + 2mm), 3M heavy-duty tape

### /halbach-array — Magnet Experiments
- **Goal:** Remagnetize 3D printer beds
- **Halbach array:** Polarities oriented to concentrate field one side → FAILED (de-magnetized sheet uniformly, too weak for flexible magnets)
- **Solution:** Opposing polarity stacks (adjacent magnets oppose on single axis) — 28× 30×5mm ring magnets per assembly, 3D-printed PLA Pro housing with grip handles
